{{< badge content="Mac App Store" color="purple" icon="shopping-bag" size="lg" link="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6468120888" >}}

## What it does

Locks input for as long as the window is open, so a cloth over the keys cannot trigger keystrokes, shortcuts or clicks. Exiting takes a deliberate action the cleaning cloth will not perform.

That is the entire app, and it is the correct scope for the problem.

## Notes

Free on the App Store. macOS has no built-in equivalent short of logging out — which does not stop the trackpad either.
