## What it does

FrameBeast builds marketing assets for an app release. Screenshots go in, device frames, text, shapes, gradients and wallpapers go around them, and what comes out is the set a launch actually needs: App Store screenshots at the required sizes, one localised set per language, OG images, GitHub social previews, X and LinkedIn headers.

Keeping them in one project is the point. Localised variants live beside the original rather than in a parallel folder structure, so changing a layout does not mean redoing eleven languages by hand, and the compositions for the store and for social media share the same source images.

AI generation is offered as a starting point — assets produced, then edited in the same canvas — rather than as the output.

## Notes

- Free from the App Store; AI usage and bulk export are the paid tier, charged as a subscription. macOS 15 or later.
- There is no Homebrew cask, and no direct download: the App Store is the distribution.
- The vendor labels it new and is asking for feedback, which is worth knowing before a release depends on it.
