{{< lead >}}One emulator, one multiplexer, a prompt, and a pile of small Rust and Go programs that replace tools shipped in 1979.{{< /lead >}}

A stock Unix tool replaced by something with colour, a fuzzy finder and sane defaults, repeated twenty-odd times. Most of it takes one line in `~/.zshrc`.
