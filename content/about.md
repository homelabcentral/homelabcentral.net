Homelab Central is your source for everything homelab — the hardware, the
software that runs on it, and the long, rewarding journey of self-hosting.

It is a running set of notes on that journey: the machines, the services
stacked on top of them, and the parts that only become obvious after something
falls over at 3am.

Everything here is written from a setup that is actually running. Where a guide
has not been re-tested against a current release, it says so.
